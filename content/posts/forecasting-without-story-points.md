Draft notes:

- The question every team dreads: "when will it be done?" — and why estimation theatre (planning poker, re-pointing, velocity maths) doesn't really answer it.
- The alternative: count finished items per week (throughput), then run a Monte Carlo simulation — thousands of simulated futures sampled from your own history.
- You get a probability distribution, not a date: "85% chance by the 14th" is a more honest sentence than "it'll be done on the 10th".
- Walk through a small worked example with real-ish numbers.
- Caveats: needs reasonably sliced work, a stable-ish team, and honesty about scope growth.
- What changed for the team when the conversation moved from estimates to probabilities.
